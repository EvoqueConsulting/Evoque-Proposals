Positioning

Four ways to position Arti.

Each answers a different question a homeowner is asking. Our suggestion: lead with 2, back it with 3, and use 4 as the voice.

Long-term direction

1 · The space planner

"What can your space actually do?"

Why it works: planning and playing with space is what she loves, and it fits her move toward architecture. The work is the hero.

Worth preparing: an answer on credentials, since it points toward architecture.

Lead with this

2 · Beautiful homes that actually get finished

"The faster you finish, the more you save."

Why it works: design with control over timelines, labour and budget. It fits her fixed fee, documented deliverables and her future app for the execution problem.

Back it with this

3 · The reinvention specialist

"Give what you own a second life."

Why it works: she is very good at refurbishing and working with what exists. A clear, less crowded niche that suits house-proud clients who want to elevate a home they already have.

Use as tone of voice

4 · Contemporary classical, client-led

"Your taste, her eye."

Why it works: warm, personal and not templated; she doesn't want a box.

Best used as: the voice rather than the lead, since on its own it is less distinctive.

Lines that could carry the idea
Design in Balance · Style. Substance. Sensibility.Rethink. Repurpose. Refine. · New potential in existing spacesGood Taste. Good Sense. · Texture. Thought. Transformation.
