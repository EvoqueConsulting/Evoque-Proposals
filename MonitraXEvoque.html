AI & automation

Make each rep do the work of two.

Set up once, run through the retainer. All content is drawn from approved evidence, with human sign-off on medical claims and no patient-identifiable data.

Pre-call brief

Per-doctor brief: specialty, history, the most relevant case story, the likely objection and its counter.

Post-visit follow-up

A 30-second rep note becomes a personalised, compliant follow-up with evidence attached, ending the passive "I'll call you."

Adoption triggers

After a first prescription, automated nudges to the doctor and alerts to the rep when an account goes quiet.

Objection & demo coach

Mock-call practice built on LAER and STEP-UP and the upBeat Academy curriculum, so messaging stays consistent.

Approved-answer assistant

Reps ask a question in the field and get answers only from the approved evidence library.

Case-story engine

Clinical wins, once anonymised and approved by the clinician, become one-pagers and snippets in days.

Plus a region and rep dashboard for prescriber funnel, activation rate and at-risk accounts. Built on Monitra's existing CRM and messaging tools, confirmed in the Phase 1 audit.

Dependencies

What keeps it on schedule.

Delivery speed rests on seven commitments from Monitra.

A named owner

One internal marketing lead who can approve content within agreed turnaround windows.

Clinical and compliance sign-off

Clinician review of evidence and claims, and a compliance check on all medical-promotion content.

One verified fact base

A single approved set of numbers (patients, studies, hospitals, revenue) before any asset goes out.

KOL and ambassador time

Clinical champions for case stories, speaker roles and the pilot; employees willing to take part; patient stories only with consent; recognition non-monetary.

Access

Admin access to website, social and LinkedIn; CRM and rep activity data. No patient-identifiable data goes into AI tools.

Field and hiring inputs

Rep time for onboarding and the South and Maharashtra pilot, and the hiring plan behind the fundraise to size employer branding.

Academy readiness

The Academy platform and faculty content are built by Monitra's cardiologist, with a launch date fixed early so our launch plan can run to it.
