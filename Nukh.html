01 · Market Reality

The market
you're entering

A crowded, trust-driven category where first impressions are everything — and hesitation is the enemy of conversion.

"No positioning → No differentiation → No recall → No repeat → Dependence on ads."
01
High Return Rates
Online footwear sees 35–40% returns. Without try-before-you-buy, the first purchase is driven entirely by perception — not product quality.
02
The Crowded Middle
₹1,200–₹1,500 is the most contested zone. Without a clear USP, competing here means competing on discounts — a race to the bottom.
03
Three Player Types
Legacy brands win on trust. Design-led brands win on identity. Marketplace sellers win on price. Without a lane, Nukh falls between all three.
04
The White Space
Comfortable, good-looking footwear that works across the entire day — not just occasions. That gap is real. Nobody owns it yet.
